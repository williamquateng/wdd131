Cape Coast Ghana

Ghana,officially the Republic of Ghana, is a country in West Africa. It is situated with the Gulf of Guinea and the Atlantic Ocean to the south, and shares borders with Côte d'Ivoire to the west, Burkina Faso to the north, and Togo to the east. Cape Coast is a historic coastal city in Ghana, known for its rich cultural heritage and colonial architecture.
